awesome-skills / gtm-mavericks

Six legendary marketers walk into a workflow. They argue. The disagreements are preserved.

gtm-mavericks runs your go-to-market strategy through a debate panel of Draper, Jobs, Ogilvy, Clow, Halbert, and Dunford. They critique your ICP. They fight over positioning. Forks that don't resolve are preserved as strategic choices with real tradeoffs — not smoothed into average. The output is ship-ready and audit-friendly.

01How it runs

30 minutes of real GTM rigor, on a server, while you're somewhere else.

1 · Intake

Short conversation in your agent — what's the product, who's the buyer if you know, which mode (launching new / repositioning / campaign). Then the workflow takes over.

2 · Discovery

Anthropic's web search browses the market and pulls customer signals from your corpus URLs or files. Real quotes with citable sources — not "imagine your customer thinking…"

3 · The panel

Six personas, each in their own parallel LLM task, each instructed to apply only their operating principles. Draper goes hard on emotion. Jobs reduces to one sentence. Ogilvy demands a proof per claim. The synthesis loop probes adversarially until satisfied.

4 · The deliverable

Three voices draft every asset in parallel — Dunford, Halbert, Ogilvy — a judge LLM picks the strongest, and the losers stay in the appendix for comparison. Rendered to 3-tier markdown + PDF inside Conductor. No local LaTeX.

02The panel

Each persona is a structured operating system. Not a vibe-based costume.

Every persona file at references/personas/<name>.md defines operating_principles, evaluation_questions, signature_moves, anti_patterns, voice_samples, and panel_contribution. The workflow applies them mechanically.

03From a real run

What disagreement looks like when Draper, Halbert, and Dunford actually argue.

Excerpted verbatim from a workflow run on a craft chocolate subscription (Mode B reposition). This is what the panel produces when it doesn't smooth to average: concrete tradeoffs the operator can reason about.

" Strategic fork · First Purchase Offer

Single Bar vs. Tasting Flight

A
$18 single bar with full-refund guarantee.
If you don't taste the difference from Dandelion in the first bite, keep the bar and we refund. Slower subscriber acquisition. Most buyers need 2–3 purchases before subscribing, extending CAC payback from 1 month to 3–4. Lower AOV means more transactions to break even. Guarantee claim rate is real risk at industry-typical 15–20%.
B
$39 three-bar tasting flight with guarantee.
Three distinct origins with side-by-side tasting guide. $39 feels steeper to ICPs who "don't want another $18 mistake" (now asking for a $39 mistake). Decision paralysis on origin selection. Guarantee exposure is 3×.
Recommendation · Option A
04Try it

Paste any of these into your agent. The intake takes it from there.

  • New product · no traction yet

    "let's run gtm for FlightCalm — iOS app using Apple Watch HRV to trigger mid-flight breathing exercises for anxious flyers. 4 interview transcripts in ~/Desktop/interviews/."

  • Repositioning · B2B SaaS at a plateau

    "we sell Hublink. $8M ARR Series B. Win rate dropped 15% YoY against Linear/Notion. Demos land, deals stall. Reposition."

  • Launch campaign · positioning fixed

    "Earnpay Advance — instant earned-wage access for hourly workers in 12 states. Positioning is already approved. Run the campaign mode."

  • Mid-run check-in

    "where are we on the gtm run?"

  • Halt for review

    "pause the run, I want to look at the ICP before positioning starts"

  • Resume across sessions

    "pause the run, I'll be back tomorrow" → next day, fresh session → "where were we?"

05What you get

Deliverables land in gtm-output/<run-id>/

  • gtm-full.md
    gtm-full.pdf
    The assembled 3-tier doc — Executive Summary, Part 1 Deliverables, Part 2 Appendix with panel disagreements and the decisions log. PDF generated server-side; no local LaTeX needed.
  • bundle.json
    All artifacts as structured JSON for downstream tooling. Includes non-winning voice variants for each asset, so you can compare what the judge picked against what it skipped.
  • executive_summary.json
    TL;DR, key decisions, 90-day action plan, KPIs, gaps and risks. Every line traces back through the decisions log to a panel critique or strategic fork.
  • gtm-deck.pdf
    gtm-deck.html
    Slide deck — only if you ask for it after the run. Slides render via scripts/render_slides.sh (requires marp).
