## Synthesis rules

1. **Dunford's five-component canvas is the chassis** for recommended_positioning. Every component is required:
   - `for`: the specific buyer from the ICP (pull from icp.primary_icp.demographic — do NOT paraphrase generically)
   - `who_struggles_with`: concrete struggle (use icp.primary_icp.pain_in_their_words or specific failure modes)
   - `our_product`: actual product name from ${product.name}
   - `is_a`: the category — chosen deliberately, named explicitly
   - `that`: unique value to the buyer (their value, not ours)
   - `unlike`: the named alternative(s) — use icp.primary_icp.current_alternative; never write "competitors" or "alternatives" generically

2. **Category design is half the job.** Don't skip it. category_design.category_name + category_design.frame_of_reference. The frame of reference explains what existing category the buyer would otherwise place us in, and why your chosen frame is more advantageous. 200–400 words for frame_of_reference is appropriate — this is where you do real work.

3. **Strategic forks are required.** Find at least 3 forks, ideally 5. Each fork is a real choice the operator must make — typical examples:
   - Emotional frame vs operational frame
   - Category creation vs category entry / incumbent contrast
   - Brand-first vs direct-response
   - Single brand name vs multi-product architecture
   - Lead message register: fear vs aspiration vs operational
   - Primary motion: OSS-led PLG vs direct enterprise sales
   For EACH fork: option_a and option_b, each with label, positioning (one paragraph), tradeoff (REAL consequences — capped growth, slower cycle, higher CAC, brand risk — not vibes). recommendation: "a" or "b" — make the call.

4. **Tradeoffs must name consequences, not aesthetics.** Bad: "Option A: more emotional. Option B: more rational." Good: "Option A positions us as a challenger and requires founder-led storytelling that doesn't scale to a 20-rep sales team. Option B accelerates pipeline but caps our maximum valuation multiple at the level of vendors in this category, around 4-8x revenue."

5. **Anti-failure modes:**
   - recommended_positioning.for is generic ("B2B teams") → FAIL. Pull from ICP.
   - recommended_positioning.unlike is generic ("the alternatives") → FAIL. Name them.
   - strategic_forks is empty or fewer than 3 → FAIL. Look harder; real panels of 6 personas disagree.
   - Recommendation is "either could work" → FAIL. Take a position.
   - category_name is "[Product] Platform" or any obvious tautology → FAIL. The category exists or doesn't independent of us.
