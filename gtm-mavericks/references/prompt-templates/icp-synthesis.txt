## Synthesis rules

1. **Dunford's structure is the chassis.** primary_icp MUST include: demographic, psychographic, trigger_event, current_alternative, pain_in_their_words. All 5 required, all populated with real specifics.

2. **Color with the others:**
   - Draper sharpens psychographic (what they want to FEEL, who they want to BE seen as)
   - Jobs sharpens the experience trigger and the self-image dimension
   - Ogilvy demands pain_in_their_words be a real quote when discovery contains one (otherwise paraphrase in their idiom)
   - Clow asks whether this buyer wants to publicly associate with the product (cultural identity)
   - Halbert demands trigger_event be specific enough to write a cold-email subject line for

3. **Anti-template guarantees:**
   - demographic is NOT "B2B leaders" — it's role + company stage + size + situation
   - pain_in_their_words is NOT "we need to be more efficient" — it's a specific quote in the buyer's idiom
   - trigger_event is NOT "evaluating new tools" — it's a specific event with specific consequences
   - current_alternative is NOT "doing nothing" alone — name the actual tools, the actual scripts, the actual workaround
   - If the product context is known (e.g., a developer infra product), the demographic MUST be role-specific (engineer, architect, VP Eng) — NOT generic "ops leader"

4. **Surface disagreement, don't bury it.** panel_disagreements is where the operator makes their real call at the gate. Find at least 3, ideally 5, real splits in the panel inputs. For each: list each persona's view (only those who weighed in) and your synthesis_recommendation. The recommendation must take a position — "could go either way" is failure.

5. **Secondary ICPs (up to 2):** Only if the panel surfaced legitimate alternative ICPs. Same chassis: demographic, psychographic, trigger_event, current_alternative, pain_in_their_words. Label each (e.g., "VP of Engineering — The Approver Who Is Quietly Terrified").

6. **Rejected candidates:** ICPs the panel considered and ruled out. Each: icp (one-line label) + why_not (specific reason citing a persona).

## Self-check before output

Scan your draft for these failure signals and fix before emitting:
- Any field containing `[placeholder]` or `${var}` substrings
- demographic missing one of: role, company stage, size, situation
- pain_in_their_words is one sentence of marketing speak instead of a buyer quote
- panel_disagreements is empty or all show consensus (look harder — real panels disagree)
