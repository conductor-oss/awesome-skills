# Rubric: Persona Voice Fidelity

## Why this matters

The skill's differentiator is committing one persona's voice to asset generation. If you can swap "voice_persona: draper" for "voice_persona: dunford" without the copy meaningfully changing, the entire panel exercise was theater.

This rubric grades whether the assets' voice actually matches the persona claimed.

## Inputs you'll see

- `artifacts` — up to 2 sampled artifacts with their `voice_persona` field
- `voice_personas_present` — list of voice personas the run committed to

## Reference: what each persona's voice sounds like

- **draper** — emotional, feeling-first, headline-before-body, reframes objections as virtues. "It's toasted." Refuses bullet lists. Story over proof.
- **jobs** — reductive, one-sentence-friendly, names the enemy. Lead with what the user *feels*. Kills jargon.
- **ogilvy** — research-led, long-copy, proof-per-claim, respects the reader. Specifics over generalities. "Loudest noise is the electric clock."
- **clow** — cultural manifesto, challenger brand frame, "would someone share this." Brand at the center, product at the edges.
- **halbert** — direct-response, offer-first, urgency, P.S. closing, "Dear Friend" framing. Proof stack. CTA every paragraph.
- **dunford** — operational rigor, alternatives-first, five-component canvas, 30-second pitch, sales-narrative test. Specific buyer + specific alternative + specific value.

## Criteria

Grade on a 1-5 scale:

- **5 — Exceptional.** Voice is unmistakable. If you swap the persona name on the artifact, a knowledgeable reader could re-identify the actual voice from the copy alone.
- **4 — Good.** Voice is distinct. A few sections lean into the persona's signature moves visibly.
- **3 — Acceptable.** Voice is consistent but not pronounced. Could be 2-3 personas with minor tweaks.
- **2 — Weak.** Voice is mostly generic with one or two surface tells (a Draper-ish headline, then standard SaaS copy).
- **1 — Failed.** Voice is corporate-neutral. Persona claim is unsupported by the copy.

## Pass condition

Score ≥ 3.5 across samples.

## Test you can run mentally

Read the artifact's `body` or `subject`. Ask: would I bet $100 that the voice listed is correct? If you would only bet $20, score 3. If you'd refuse the bet, score 1-2.
